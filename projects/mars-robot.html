Makerspace Autonomous Robot.

A ROS 2 autonomous robot built around Jetson Orin Nano compute, RPLiDAR A1 ranging, and ZED 2i vision, with my focus on the electrical and hardware integration.

TeamIEEE RAS Industry Team
RoleElectrical Lead
Timeline2024–Present
PlatformROS 2

System

The Makerspace Autonomous Robot is a ROS 2-based mobile robotics platform. The system combines onboard edge compute with LiDAR and stereo vision for autonomous-robot development.

  • Jetson Orin Nano for onboard compute.
  • RPLiDAR A1 for range sensing.
  • ZED 2i for vision.
  • ROS 2 as the robotics software framework.

My contribution

I led the hardware-integration side of the project: power distribution, motor drivers, wiring, and chassis assembly. In the IEEE RAS electrical-lead role, I also designed and soldered circuit boards, managed the robot’s data bus and power wiring, developed Teensy firmware, and worked on ROS nodes for control.
